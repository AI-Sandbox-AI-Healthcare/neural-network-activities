Stage 2 — Where Does Saturation Hit Hardest in BPTT?

BPTT unrolls the RNN and multiplies Jacobians: ∂h⁽ᵛ⁾/∂h⁽ᵛ⁻¹⁾ = diag(1−h²)·W. Going back T steps multiplies T−1 of these together.
