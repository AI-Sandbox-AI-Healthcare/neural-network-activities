💡 Architectural Insight
Bias is not special — it is a weight connected to a constant input of 1. PyTorch and most frameworks store W and b together for exactly this reason.

What You Just Did
  • Bias c is just a weight connected to a constant input of 1.
  • Adding a column of 1s to X gives the bias a carrier that fires for every sample.
  • Stacking c as a new bottom row in W folds the bias into the weight matrix.
  • This is called bias absorption — used internally by frameworks like PyTorch.
