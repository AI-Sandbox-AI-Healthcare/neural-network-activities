Why Does Cross-Entropy Matter?
Step 1 of 5
📐 Punishes Confident Mistakes
If the model is 99% sure of the wrong answer, cross-entropy makes that mistake cost a lot. Being confidently wrong is punished much more than being only a little off.
∂ Easy to Learn From
Paired with softmax, the update rule is simple: prediction − truth. The model just compares its guess to the right answer and nudges itself in that direction.
📊 Measures "Surprise"
Think of it as a surprise score. A confident, correct guess isn't surprising at all (low cost). A confident, wrong guess is very surprising (high cost).
