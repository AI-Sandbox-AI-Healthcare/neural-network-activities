Stage 3 — A Seq2Seq Reformulation

In seq2seq NER the model generates a structured markup sequence. The output is typically shorter than the input — only entities are emitted, with type tags.

Input:
Óscar   Romero   was   born   in   El   Salvador
Seq2Seq output:
<PER> Óscar Romero </PER> was born in <LOC> El Salvador </LOC>
