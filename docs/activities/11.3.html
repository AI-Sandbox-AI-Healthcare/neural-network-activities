Takeaway
  • Two gaps, two problems. Human − train is the bias you could still remove (Task A: 35 points). Train − dev is variance (Task B: 40 points).
  • Learning curves are plotted against training-set size. Training accuracy falls and dev accuracy rises until they meet. If they meet far below the target, as Task A's do at about 54%, more data cannot help.
  • Goodfellow §11.3: first make the model fit its training set (a bigger model, tuned optimization). Gather more data only when it does and dev lags behind (Task B); regularization and augmentation are cheaper alternatives.
  • Data needs grow on a log scale: Task B needs 16× the data for +16 points. If large, well-tuned models still cannot fit the training set, suspect the data itself (noisy labels or missing information).
