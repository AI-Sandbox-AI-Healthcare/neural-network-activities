Optimizer Race

Surface: f(x,y) = (x−3)² + 8(y−2)². Start: (1, 3). Minimum: (3, 2). This ill-conditioned bowl is 8× steeper in y than x — a natural stress-test for optimizers.

🏔️
Ill-conditioned surfaces

When one direction is much steeper, a uniform step size overshoots the steep dim while crawling in the flat one — causing zigzags.

📐
Adaptive lr fixes this

AdaGrad, RMSProp, and Adam maintain per-parameter running statistics to scale each coordinate's step independently.

⚠️
Each has failure modes

AdaGrad's denominator grows forever and stalls. Momentum can overshoot. Adam needs more hyperparameter tuning.

The four optimizers

SGD
W←W−η·g

One lr for all. Zigzags on elongated surfaces.

AdaGrad
s←s+g²; W←W−ηg/√s

Adapts then stalls — denom grows forever.

RMSProp
s←ρs+(1−ρ)g²; W←W−ηg/√s

EMA keeps denom bounded. Smooth path.

Adam
m̂/v̂ bias-corrected EMA

Momentum + adaptive rate. Most direct path.
